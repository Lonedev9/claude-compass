# Msheireb, 2010–2020 — treatment

Style: Satellite Briefing. 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~56 s. English analyst voice (Kokoro, offline), burned-in transcript strip plus `.srt`. No logos.

## 1. Three structures, one choice

A. **Time rings in a locked frame.** One nadir frame over the site, eleven dated Landsat plates scanned in year by year. The cadence of the scans carries the story: slow at clearing and excavation, fast through construction, one silence before the last year. Then a climb out.
B. **Two places at one scale.** Msheireb against its neighbour Souq Waqif, whip-panning between them each year: one rebuilt from scratch, one restored. It's a strong contrast, but the brief is Msheireb's own decade, and at 15 m the souq barely changes from year to year.
C. **Measurement-led.** The camera travels the site's edges with range lines (Al Rayyan Road frontage, west–east span), and dates come in as side readings. That's precise, but the change over time becomes secondary.

**Choice: A.** The imagery itself tells the story: pale cleared ground in 2010, a dark pit spreading in 2011–12, one excavation filling the whole outline in 2013, a block grid rising from 2015, complete by 2020. One range line from C (the 625 m Al Rayyan Road frontage) sets the scale before time runs.

- Opening image: a black frame that a scan line paints in with the February 2010 plate, already at the site's altitude. No dive from orbit.
- Ending: a climb from the site to the Qatar peninsula, with the reticle left on one point.
- Score shape: dry telemetry (ticks, pings, pulse bass), getting faster through the construction years. A suck-out before 2020. One wet timbre (bowed metal) when colour returns.

## 2. Logline and arc

In ten years of free satellite pictures, a 31-hectare quarter of old Doha is cleared, dug out as one hole and rebuilt as a new district.

- Setup: 2010, pale cleared ground beside Souq Waqif. The outline and its scale are measured.
- Turn: 2013, one excavation fills the outline. Everything after this point is new.
- Ending: 2020 regains its colour inside the outline only, and the camera climbs away until the district is a single point on the peninsula.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** NASA Earth Observatory's *World of Change*: one locked frame, dates as the only cut, time spacing as rhythm. And open-source investigation annotation: thin lines, one claim per screen, the source named under every number.
- **Don't take:** their layouts, typefaces, music or any specific shot. They're never named in the film.

## 9. Data honesty

- Landsat 7 ETM+ (2010–12, SLC-off: two to four same-year dates composited) and Landsat 8 OLI (2013–20), pan-sharpened to 15 m. All from the USGS archive via Google Cloud public data.
- The outline was traced by hand on the 2020 and 2013 plates (32.2 ha). The published figure is 31 ha. Both appear on screen.
- No building is drawn. Dates come from public sources (FACTS.md).
